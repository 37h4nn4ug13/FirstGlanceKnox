# Cleaner instruction manual

[All manuals](START-HERE.md) · [Customer response guide](customer-interactions.md)

Your job is to review the work offered, complete the accepted scope, report problems promptly, and give the office enough information to approve completion. Your fixed job pay is separate from the customer's invoice.

## 1. Start your day

1. Sign in and open **Overview**.
2. Check **Notifications / Inbox**, **My jobs** or **Jobs & offers**, and **Schedule**.
3. Review new offers and accepted appointments before traveling.
4. Read the connection notice. Open necessary job details while online; the offline shell only retains limited summaries.

## 2. Review and answer a job offer

1. Open an offer from **Jobs & offers**, Overview, or Notifications.
2. Check the property, arrival/finish time, scope, safety requirements, instructions, response deadline, and **Agreed fixed job pay**.
3. If you can do the work on those terms, select **Accept this offer**.
4. If you cannot, add a useful reason and select **Decline**. For questions about time, scope, or pay, contact the office before accepting.
5. After acceptance, select **Open job details**.

Acceptance records your exact agreement. It creates a pending pay entry; it does not mean you have been paid. You cannot accept an expired, withdrawn, or superseded offer. If the appointment changes, review and accept the new offer—your old acceptance does not cover the new time automatically.

## 3. Work through the appointment

Open the job and review its scope, access instructions, hazards, and checklist. Use **Open directions** for navigation and **Call customer** when a phone number is available and the call is needed for the visit.

Under **Your next step**, update the job as it happens:

| Button | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **On my way** | You are leaving for this job. |
| **I have arrived** | You have reached the property. |
| **Start work** | You are ready to begin the agreed work. |
| **Report an issue** | A problem prevents normal progress or needs office attention. Enter details. |
| **Resume work** | The issue has been resolved and work can continue. |
| **Submit completion** | The agreed work and required checklist/evidence are complete. |

Buttons depend on the current status. Do not advance the job early merely to clear it from your list. These status changes are internal records; they are not a guarantee that the customer received a text or email.

## 4. Talk to the customer at arrival

Introduce yourself and confirm the quoted scope and access: “I'm here for the window-cleaning service on your estimate. Before I start, is there anything I should know about access or the areas we're cleaning?”

If the customer requests extra windows, additional services, a discount, or a different appointment, ask the office to confirm the change. Do not promise pricing or quietly expand the agreed scope. Record relevant details in the job notes.

If access is unavailable or conditions prevent safe work, describe the issue and contact the office. Do not mark inaccessible work complete. The administrator handles scheduling/cancellation decisions.

## 5. Add notes and private evidence

1. Enter useful progress or issue information in **Job notes or issue details** when submitting a status change.
2. Under **Job notes & evidence**, choose a photo in **Add a private job photo**.
3. Add a clear caption, such as the window/location and what the image demonstrates.
4. Select **Upload photo** and confirm it appears.

Supported uploads are JPEG, PNG, or WebP, up to 10 MB. Photos require a connection and are not stored in the offline outbox. Job evidence stays private; it is not automatically posted to the public gallery. Send any proposed marketing use to the office for its separate approval process.

## 6. Submit completion and handle corrections

1. Check every required checklist item after doing the work.
2. Add final notes and required photos.
3. Select **Submit completion**.
4. Confirm the job is awaiting review. The office must approve it.

If the office returns the job for correction, read the correction in the notes, clarify anything unclear, complete the requested work, and submit again. Do not assume the return changes your fixed-pay agreement; ask the office about any disputed terms.

When speaking to the customer, use: “The agreed work is finished. I'll submit the details to the office, and they'll handle the final review and invoice.” Do not issue a bill or mark a payment in the cleaner workspace.

## 7. Understand your earnings

Open **My earnings** and read **Cleaner job pay**.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | You accepted the offer; the work has not yet been approved for payment. |
| Payable | The administrator approved completion. The agreed amount is ready for office payout processing. |
| Paid | The office recorded the external payment. |
| Reversed | The agreement/entry was reversed, such as after withdrawal or rescheduling. Ask the office if the reason is unclear. |

Your pay does not become a percentage of the customer's invoice. A customer's partial payment does not determine whether approved cleaner pay is payable. Report discrepancies with the job number and agreed amount; the cleaner screen cannot change ledger entries.

## 8. Offline use and finishing your shift

An eligible status form already open on the device can queue an update offline. The notice will say it is saved to this device only. A cold offline visit may show only the limited assignment-summary shell, not the full job or customer details.

Reconnect with the same account and wait for synchronization success. If the office changed the job while you were offline, the server may reject the old update. Open **Device outbox**, review the rejected update, and report the error and actual work performed instead of repeatedly submitting conflicting statuses. Once the office confirms the old update should not be applied, choose **Discard this update** and confirm. This removes the queued copy only; it does not undo a server update.

Before signing out, synchronize all queued updates, confirm uploads, report unresolved issues, and check for correction requests. Sign-out is blocked while the device outbox contains pending updates; synchronize or resolve them first. Customer billing questions and appointment changes go to the administrator.
